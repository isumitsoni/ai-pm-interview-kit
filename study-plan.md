# 7-Day AI PM Interview Study Plan

Use the five prompt files plus `real-questions.md` as a full-week rep cycle. The goal is not to memorize polished answers. The goal is to build faster structure, sharper tradeoff language, and a repeatable answer bank.

---

## Day 1: Baseline and story inventory

- Read `README.md` and `real-questions.md` once end to end.
- Build a story bank with 8 examples from your own work:
  - launch with imperfect quality
  - tradeoff between speed and trust
  - prioritization under weak evidence
  - cross-functional conflict
  - customer insight that changed roadmap
  - metric design decision
  - incident or failure recovery
  - build versus buy decision
- End by writing 3 weaknesses you already see in your answers.

**Output:** one-page story bank plus a list of weak spots.

---

## Day 2: Behavioral reps

- Read `behavioral-prompts.md`.
- Run all 3 practice prompts in Claude.
- Answer each once out loud, once in writing.
- Rewrite your weakest answer using STAR-T.
- Pull 4 behavioral-style questions from `real-questions.md` and do lightning reps.

**Output:** 3 improved behavioral answer outlines.

---

## Day 3: Product design reps

- Read `product-design-prompts.md`.
- Run 2 full mock cases and 1 short case.
- For every case, force yourself to define:
  - one user
  - one value moment
  - one trust risk
  - one MVP
- Finish with 4 design questions from `real-questions.md`.

**Output:** 2 reusable design frameworks and 1 clean MVP answer template.

---

## Day 4: Prioritization reps

- Read `prioritization-prompts.md`.
- Run 3 prioritization cases.
- In every answer, explicitly say what you are deprioritizing and why.
- Pull 4 prioritization questions from `real-questions.md`.
- Time-box at least one answer to 7 minutes to practice executive brevity.

**Output:** one prioritization template you can reuse in interviews.

---

## Day 5: Tradeoff reps

- Read `ai-tradeoffs-prompts.md`.
- Run 3 cases on model choice, architecture, and human review.
- Build one reusable checklist:
  - quality
  - latency
  - cost
  - trust
  - reversibility
- End with 4 tradeoff questions from `real-questions.md`.

**Output:** one tradeoff checklist and 2 polished answer outlines.

---

## Day 6: Metrics reps

- Read `metrics-prompts.md`.
- Run 3 cases.
- Force each answer to include:
  - primary value metric
  - supporting behavior metric
  - quality metric
  - guardrail
- End with all 4 metrics questions from `real-questions.md`.

**Output:** one metrics framework plus 3 example metric stacks.

---

## Day 7: Full loop simulation

- Run a 60-minute mock interview:
  - 1 behavioral
  - 1 product design
  - 1 prioritization
  - 1 tradeoff
  - 1 metrics
- Use `real-questions.md` only for prompts.
- Review the recording or transcript.
- Rewrite only the 2 weakest answers. Do not rewrite all 5.
- End by writing a final 1-page cheat sheet with your best structures and strongest stories.

**Output:** final cheat sheet for interview week.

---

## Rep cadence rule

If time is tight, do fewer questions and more rewrites. One weak answer rewritten well is worth more than five rushed reps.
