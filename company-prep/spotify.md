# Spotify — AI PM Interview Prep

## What the company cares about

- Mission is "music for everyone" — the listener is always the primary user, but the creator/artist ecosystem is equally important and often overlooked by candidates
- Squad model (bands, tribes, guilds) is a cultural identity, not just an org chart. Cross-functional ownership and autonomy matter in answers
- Experimentation culture is deeply embedded — Spotify A/B tests at scale across markets and user segments
- AI and ML are core to the product, not a layer on top. Recommendations, discovery, and playlist generation are the product
- Podcasting is a major strategic bet — discovery, transcription, recommendations, and creator monetization are active investment areas

## 5-7 targeted questions

**1. How would you improve podcast discovery on Spotify?**
Why this is hard: Podcasting is a different consumption model than music. Relevance signals differ, completion rates matter differently, and the supply is chaotic. You have to design for both new-listener acquisition and long-form engagement without conflating them.

**2. How would you define and measure trust in AI DJ?**
Why this is hard: AI DJ is an autonomous, hosted experience. Trust is multidimensional: trust in music taste, trust in pacing, trust in tone. Defining a single metric is impossible. Candidates who reach for one number miss the design complexity.

**3. Design a mood-detection feature for Spotify. How would you decide what moods to support?**
Why this is hard: The product decision is less about the model and more about what Spotify should infer vs ask. Mood detection from behavior feels clever until you think about what happens when it is wrong and how users react when the product labels their emotional state incorrectly.

**4. Spotify Discover Weekly has been declining in open rate among heavy users. What do you do?**
Why this is hard: It could be product fatigue, recommendation staleness, changing usage patterns, or competition from AI DJ and Daylist. Candidates need to distinguish between a metric problem, a product problem, and a market shift before jumping to solutions.

**5. How do you improve recommendation quality for niche or non-mainstream genres?**
Why this is hard: Cold-start and long-tail distribution problems require data strategy, not just ML tuning. The right answer has a position on what Spotify owes artists in niche genres and how to balance mainstream popularity signals against deep-catalog discovery.

**6. How would you define success for AI-generated playlist creation?**
Why this is hard: Success depends on whether Spotify wants to replace manual playlist curation, complement it, or drive new listening behaviors. Those three goals produce different metrics. Candidates who collapse them into one retention metric are missing the strategic layer.

**7. How would you use AI to improve creator tools on Spotify for podcast hosts?**
Why this is hard: Creator-side product is underweighted in most candidate prep. The question tests whether you can reason about supply-side incentives, creator retention, and how Spotify's business model connects creator success to listener outcomes.

## What to emphasize in your answers

- Both sides of the marketplace: listener and creator/artist. Most candidates design for listeners and forget supply entirely
- Data and experimentation rigor: Spotify will expect specific success metrics and a credible theory of how to test your idea
- Cross-functional collaboration: name the specific teams you would involve and what each one owns
- Long-term user trust over short-term engagement metrics
- The specific mechanics of AI behavior in the product, not just "use ML to personalize"

## What to avoid

- Treating Spotify as just a music player. It is a discovery engine, a creator platform, and an audio network
- Vague metrics like "engagement" without specifying what behavior you are actually measuring
- Ignoring the podcasting and creator ecosystem, which is where significant AI investment is going
- Proposing features without thinking about the trust implications of AI-generated or AI-influenced content
- Answers that treat AI DJ and Discover Weekly as interchangeable
