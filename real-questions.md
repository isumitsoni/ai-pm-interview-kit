Notes:
- Only companies with more than 100 employees were included.
- `Direct` means the question or very close wording appeared in the source.
- `Inferred` means the source clearly described the interview theme, but not the full question verbatim.

## Key takeaways

- OpenAI-style loops skew toward product design, metrics, and tradeoffs in the same round.
- Enterprise AI companies surface more questions on architecture choice, GTM realism, and rollout judgment.
- Blind is useful as a company-signal source, but most public question detail still comes from Glassdoor and LinkedIn writeups.
- All source URLs marked `[UNVERIFIED]` — verify current links before citing publicly.
